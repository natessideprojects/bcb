# Audio sources

- Music: Nathan-supplied `Fairground Fun.mp3`.
- Voice: Nathan-supplied ElevenLabs Grant announcer take, split into event clips by Claude.
- Crowd: Nathan-supplied cheering, shouting and disappointment recordings.
- Effects: trimmed and mixed FilmCow Recorded SFX. Official source and usage terms: https://filmcow.itch.io/filmcow-sfx (checked November 10, 2026). FilmCow permits royalty-free use in personal and commercial projects under the restrictions stated there. The sounds are not CC0; their original terms remain applicable.
- Older synthesized WAVs remain available for historical assets; current playback uses the MP3 manifest.

Build helpers in scripts/audio document the transformation. Paths originally referenced Claude's build environment; the shipped clips do not depend on those paths or a live generation service. Supplied music and voice were integrated as requested; account-level generation licensing was not independently audited.
